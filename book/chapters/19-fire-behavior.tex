\chapter{Fire behavior}\label{ch:firebasics}

A wildland fire spreads because burning fuel heats the unburned fuel ahead of it to ignition. How fast the front
moves, its \emph{rate of spread}, depends on three things:
\begin{itemize}
  \item the fuel: its load, depth, particle size and moisture;
  \item the wind;
  \item the slope.
\end{itemize}
WRF-Fire uses the semi-empirical model of \citet{rothermel1972}, with fuel descriptions from \citet{anderson1982} and
\citet{scott2005}. This chapter describes the model as WRF-Fire codes it in \code{phys/module_fr_fire_phys.F}; the
numerics that move the front are in \cref{ch:fire}.

\section{Fuel models}

A fuel model is a set of numbers describing a type of vegetation as fuel:
\begin{itemize}
  \item the fuel load $w_0$;
  \item the fuel bed depth $\delta$;
  \item the surface-area-to-volume ratio of the particles $\sigma$ (\code{savr});
  \item the moisture of extinction $M_x$, above which the fuel does not burn (\code{fuelmce});
  \item the particle density, mineral content and heat content.
\end{itemize}
WRF-Fire's built-in table (\code{module_fr_fire_phys.F}, the defaults read through \code{namelist.fire}) has 53
categories:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Anderson's 13 models;
  \item a no-fuel category;
  \item the 40 models of Scott and Burgan, which are mapped onto the table's indices.
\end{itemize}
The fire grid of the case takes its categories from LANDFIRE maps (\code{NFUEL_CAT}). The fuel moisture of the case
is a uniform 8\,\% (\code{fuelmc_g = 0.08}).

\section{Rothermel's rate of spread}

Rothermel writes the rate of spread on flat ground without wind as the ratio of the heat received by the fuel ahead
of the front to the heat needed to ignite it:
\begin{equation}
  R_0 = \frac{I_R\,\xi}{\rho_b\,\varepsilon\,Q_{ig}} .
\end{equation}
At initialization, \src{phys/module_fr_fire_phys.F}{set_fire_params} computes every factor per fire-grid cell from
the fuel model of that cell. The factors are in English units, as in Rothermel's paper:
\begin{align*}
  \beta &= \frac{w_0}{\delta\,\rho_p} && \text{packing ratio (\code{betafl})}, \\
  \beta_{\mathrm{op}} &= 3.348\,\sigma^{-0.8189} && \text{optimum packing ratio}, \\
  \Gamma_{\max} &= \frac{\sigma^{1.5}}{495 + 0.0594\,\sigma^{1.5}}, \qquad
  A = \frac{1}{4.774\,\sigma^{0.1} - 7.27} && \text{reaction velocities}, \\
  \Gamma &= \Gamma_{\max}\left(\frac{\beta}{\beta_{\mathrm{op}}}\right)^{A}
            \exp\!\Bigl(A\Bigl(1 - \frac{\beta}{\beta_{\mathrm{op}}}\Bigr)\Bigr), \\
  \eta_M &= 1 - 2.59\,r_M + 5.11\,r_M^2 - 3.52\,r_M^3, \quad r_M = M_f/M_x && \text{moisture damping}, \\
  \eta_s &= 0.174\,S_e^{-0.19} && \text{mineral damping}, \\
  I_R &= \Gamma\,w_n\,h\,\eta_M\,\eta_s && \text{reaction intensity}, \\
  \xi &= \frac{\exp\bigl((0.792 + 0.681\,\sigma^{0.5})(\beta + 0.1)\bigr)}{192 + 0.2595\,\sigma}
       && \text{propagating flux ratio}, \\
  \varepsilon &= \exp(-138/\sigma), \qquad Q_{ig} = 250 + 1116\,M_f && \text{heating number, heat of pre-ignition}.
\end{align*}
Here $w_n$ is the net fuel load (without minerals), $h$ the heat content and $\rho_b$ the bulk density.

Wind and slope multiply the base rate:
\begin{equation}
  R = R_0\,(1 + \phi_w + \phi_s), \qquad
  \phi_w = C\,U^{B}\left(\frac{\beta}{\beta_{\mathrm{op}}}\right)^{-E}, \qquad
  \phi_s = 5.275\,\beta^{-0.3}\,\tan^2\varphi,
\end{equation}
with
\begin{equation}
  C = 7.47\exp\!\bigl(-0.133\,\sigma^{0.55}\bigr), \quad B = 0.02526\,\sigma^{0.54}, \quad
  E = 0.715\exp\!\bigl(-3.59\times10^{-4}\sigma\bigr).
\end{equation}
In these formulas:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $U$ is the midflame wind speed in ft/min, the component normal to the fire front;
  \item $\varphi$ is the slope angle in the direction normal to the front.
\end{itemize}
The cell-dependent parts are stored per cell ($R_0$ as \code{r_0}, $B$ as \code{bbb}, the factor in front of $U^B$ as
\code{phiwc}). At every step only the powers of the current wind are evaluated.

\section{The rate of spread at run time}

\src{phys/module_fr_fire_phys.F}{fire_ros} evaluates the spread rate at a point of the front from the wind and slope
normal to the front:
\begin{algorithm}[ht]
\caption{\code{fire_ros} for one point (Rothermel branch, \code{ibeh = 1}).}
\label{alg:ros}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\State $U \gets \min(\max(u_n, 0), 30\ \text{m/s}) \times 196.85$ \Comment{to ft/min}
\State $\phi_w \gets U^{B}\cdot$\code{phiwc}; \quad $\phi_s \gets 5.275\,\beta^{-0.3}\tan^2\varphi$ (if the slope is upward)
\State $R_b \gets 0.00508\,R_0$ \Comment{ft/min to m/s}; \quad $R_w \gets R_b\,\phi_w$; \quad $R_s \gets R_b\,\phi_s$
\State $R_w \gets R_w\cdot$\code{cor_wind}; \quad $R_s \gets R_s\cdot$\code{cor_slope}
\State $x \gets R_b + R_w + R_s - R_{\max}$ \Comment{$R_{\max} = 6$ m/s}
\If{$x > 0$}
  \State $R_w \gets R_w - x\,R_w/(R_w + R_s)$
  \State $R_s \gets R_s - x\,R_s/(R_w + R_s)$ \Comment{uses the already reduced $R_w$}
\EndIf
\State \Return $R_b + R_w + R_s$
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\begin{portnote}
Line 8 of \cref{alg:ros} divides by $R_w + R_s$ with the $R_w$ already reduced on line 7, so the cap does not split
the excess exactly in proportion. It is probably unintended, but it is what the CPU computes. A bit-for-bit port must
keep it: the GPU version of \code{fire_ros} is the same statements, compiled as a device routine
(\texttt{!\$acc routine seq}). The powers and exponentials use the reproducible library (\cref{ch:repro}).
\end{portnote}

\section{Heat release}

Behind the front, fuel burns out over a characteristic time. Before ignition the remaining fuel fraction is
$F(t) = 1$; after ignition at $t_i$ it is
\begin{equation}
  F(t) = \exp\!\left(-\frac{t - t_i}{T_f}\right),
\end{equation}
where $T_f$ is the fuel's \emph{weight}, the time for $1/e$ of the fuel to burn (\code{fgi}, \code{weight}). The heat
released is the burned fuel times its heat content. A fixed fraction of it, as moisture of the fuel and of
combustion, goes into the latent heat flux. \Cref{ch:fire} shows how $F$ is averaged over sub-cells and how the heat
enters the atmosphere.
