\chapter{Other physics}\label{ch:otherphys}

WRF has many more physics options than the case uses. This chapter gives an overview of those most relevant to fire
and regional studies, and of how each would enter the port. None of them runs in the case.

\begin{description}
  \item[Other microphysics.] Thompson (\code{mp_physics = 8}) predicts the number of rain drops and ice crystals and
    uses large lookup tables for collection. Morrison (10) is double-moment for most species. P3 (50) predicts ice
    properties continuously. NSSL schemes (17--22) are used for severe storms. All are column physics, with table
    uploads.
  \item[Other boundary layers.] MYNN (\code{bl_pbl_physics = 5}) is a TKE-based local scheme with an optional
    eddy-diffusivity mass-flux component. It is common in fire weather studies because it handles both stable and
    convective layers well. ACM2 (7) is an asymmetric convective model.
  \item[Other land models.] Noah-MP (\code{sf_surface_physics = 4}) has multiple options for canopy, snow layers,
    groundwater and dynamic vegetation. RUC (3) has thin soil layers near the surface. CLM (5) comes from climate
    modelling.
  \item[RRTMG shortwave] (\code{ra_sw_physics = 4}) uses the same correlated-$k$ method as the longwave, with 14
    bands. It pairs naturally with RRTMG LW and can share its batching on the GPU.
  \item[Slope effects on radiation] (\code{slope_rad}, \code{topo_shading}) tilt the surface toward or away from the
    sun and let ridges shade valleys. They matter in steep fire terrain such as the San Gabriel foothills of the case,
    but the case keeps them off.
  \item[Urban canopy models] (\code{sf_urban_physics}) represent buildings and streets: a single-layer model, or the
    multi-layer BEP and BEM. The out-of-bounds read of \cref{ch:pbl} comes from the BEP arrays.
  \item[Lake model] (\code{sf_lake_physics}) solves temperature profiles in lakes.
  \item[Nudging] (\code{grid_fdda}, \code{obs_nudge_opt}) relaxes the model toward analyses or observations: four-dimensional
    data assimilation by Newtonian relaxation.
  \item[Chemistry] (WRF-Chem) and \textbf{tracers} add four-dimensional arrays of species that are advected with the
    positive-definite scheme of \cref{ch:adv}. Fire emissions can feed them.
\end{description}

\section{How an option enters the port}

Each family is a phase of the extended plan (\code{roadmap/plan-4.6/stage-8-coverage.md}), with the same steps as the
case's schemes:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item a small case contract with the option on, and CPU reference runs;
  \item a kernel table of the new routines;
  \item the routine ladder of the port (\cref{ch:port});
  \item a column harness for column physics;
  \item the model-level tests.
\end{enumerate}
Until its phase is done, the startup check rejects the option with a message naming it.
