## Questions and blockers

Not blocked. Conservative choices where the rules are silent:

- The island generator names the flux4 statement function as the first executable. The pasted island is at the first real executable after the statement functions. Orders other than horizontal 5 and vertical 3, and periodic, symmetric, and polar boundaries, call wrf_error_fatal before label 99901. Open and specified boundaries are ported.
- F-STMTFN is blank in ENVIRONMENT.md. flux3, flux4, flux5, and flux6 are PURE module functions advect_v_gpu_flux3/4/5/6 with !$acc routine seq, inserted immediately after END SUBROUTINE advect_v (t_tmpl_b.F90 TMPL_NO_STMTFN). The CPU statement functions are unchanged.
- Horizontal x is two kernels (faces, then divergence), both tagged K-ADVV-X, reusing work_p2_b2_fqy3 after the y kernels. Face tests are separate IFs in source order. The east second-order face keeps the source i_end+1 and i_end expression.
- Open ys, ye, xs, and xe are kernels with no kernel id. The smoke namelist takes them. Polar zeroing after the horizontal block is not ported; polar already stopped before the island.
- The vertical flux is a private column vflux_col(WRF_KMAX+1), indexed with the source k. kts and kte are set to 0 inside the column. This assumes kts is 1 and kte is at most WRF_KMAX, which gpu_check_config enforces via e_vert.
